# Contract: the one-time test in the app (FR-009)

The page already receives the dilution model card (`docs/report/model_card.json` → `dilution`,
generated by `sentinel report`) as `model.card` in each company's page data. Nothing new is served.

## The dilution panel

Beside "the band's record per test year" (2019-2022), the panel shows the same band's record on the
one-time test: for each year from 2023 and over all, the forecast and the share that diluted, from
`card.holdout.bands` rows whose band equals the company's band.

## The model section (diagnostics)

1. "Test years: log loss (lower is better)": as now.
2. New: "The one-time test, from 2023 on (never seen while building)": the row count and the dates
   of the first and last forecast (`card.holdout.rows`, `first_row`, `last_row`), then log loss and
   AUC per level and year, and over all, from `card.holdout.results`.
3. "Calibration: forecast against what happened (all test years)": as now.
4. New: "Calibration on the one-time test": band, companies, forecast and share that diluted, from
   the `all` rows of `card.holdout.bands`.
5. The limits sentence names the test's date (`card.holdout.date`) instead of "has been run (see the
   model report)".

When `card.holdout` is absent, the panel and section show what they show today, and the sentence
says the test has not been run yet.

Every number comes from the card; none is typed into the page or its script.
