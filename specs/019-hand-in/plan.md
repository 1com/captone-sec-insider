# Implementation Plan: The hand-in (presentation, live demo, repository)

**Branch**: `019-hand-in` | **Date**: 2026-11-16 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

**Input**: Feature specification from `specs/019-hand-in/spec.md`

## Summary

Three deliverables for a 15-minute presentation with a live demo (dry run Wednesday 18 Nov) and
the public repository: about 10 slides whose every number comes from a generated number sheet
(`python -m sentinel.presentation`, from the model card and the generated reports); a written demo
path through the stock research app (026) with a spare company per step and a private backup; and
a README front page for a newcomer, with its results table checked against the model card by a
test. The app's model section gains the one-time test's record. No model, feature or label
changes. Decisions in [research.md](research.md).

**Performance Goals**: the number sheet in seconds; the app unchanged

**Constraints**: the dry run on 18 Nov; 15 minutes in all; no Tiingo-derived value in the repository
or the slides; no heavy job during the 10:00 update; light jobs only (no rebuild)

**Scale/Scope**: about 10 slides, 5-6 demo steps, one number sheet, one README front page

## Design: the slides (about 8.5 minutes, then the demo)

Numbers by id from the number sheet ([contracts/number-sheet.md](contracts/number-sheet.md)).

| # | Slide | Time | Content | Numbers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | 0:15 | "Will this company sell many new shares?" A dilution forecast for any US stock, from free public data; names | – |
| 2 | The problem | 1:00 | dilution in plain words (100 million shares, 25 million new: each old share's part falls by a fifth); who does it (small loss-making companies raising cash); how often; the signs are scattered across SEC filings | base rate |
| 3 | What we built | 1:00 | sources (SEC EDGAR, Yahoo, FRED, Cboe, FINRA, one paid month of Tiingo) into six layers, rebuilt identically and updated every weekday; four questions; the app | platform counts |
| 4 | Keeping it honest | 1:15 | no look-ahead (a quarterly report's share count is usable only from the day it was filed); the answer's definition fixed and tagged before any model; the 2023+ answers sealed, opened once on 12 Nov | – |
| 5 | Choosing the model | 0:45 | four levels (base rate, offering rule, logistic, LightGBM); test years 2019-2022, each trained only on earlier years; the simplest within one standard error; calibrated chances in five bands | test-year log loss |
| 6 | Result on the sealed test | 1:30 | bar chart of log loss per level, 2023-2026; best every year; held up against the test years; AUC in plain words; the top tenth's rate against the average | D-numbers |
| 7 | Can the chances be trusted? | 0:45 | band chart: forecast against the share that diluted, bands 1-5; slightly low at the top | band numbers |
| 8 | The other two questions | 0:45 | volatility: LightGBM equal to the ridge; results-day move: the chosen model lost to the simpler ridge; so the app serves dilution only | R-, V-numbers |
| 9 | Limits and lessons | 1:15 | the answer partly catches up with sales already announced; foreign filers left out; prices of former companies private; audits found and fixed cut price histories; unverified claims by an AI agent caught by an audit | C-, A-numbers |
| 10 | Demo, then thank you | 5:00 + 0:10 | live app (demo path); the repository link | – |

## Design: the demo path (about 5 minutes)

| Step | Role | Candidate (spare) | Shows | Time |
