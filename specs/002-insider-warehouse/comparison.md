## Explanations

Written after the run of 2026-10-23 (T031).

**No year is flagged.** With feature 001's rules put back one at a time, every year is within 3% of feature 001 for A (largest: 2022, −2.8%) and within 1.3% for B1. Totals: A −1.2%, B1 +0.1%.

How each rule moves the totals (A / B1):
- **$2 stand-in** (production → + $2): −1,660 / −763. These are events at stocks priced under $2, which the production design leaves to the market-price floor in feature 003, so it keeps them here.
- **Officer-or-director condition** (+ $2 → − O/D): +595 A, from clusters made only of 10% owners or "other" holders. B1 does not change.
- **G6 minimum, $25k → $10k** (− O/D → G6 $10,000; D46): +1,994 A but −275 B1. A purchase of $10–25k now counts, so a CEO's large buy with a director's small one becomes a two-entity cluster instead of a solo CEO/CFO event.
- **What remains** (G6 $10,000 → feature 001): A −193, B1 +4. Point-in-time entities merge owners who ever filed together into one buyer, whereas feature 001 grouped only filings that shared an owner within the window. That makes a few of feature 001's two-buyer clusters a single buyer here.
