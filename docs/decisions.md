# Decision log

Every significant choice, what else was considered, and why. v2 decisions are numbered V2-n. The
v1 log (D1–D64) was in v1's `docs/initialplan.md` §12, removed with v1 (V2-2); the v1 decisions
that still apply are summarised at the end.

| V2-13 | **Features are computed as of any trading day, for three sets of rows**: month-ends (the volatility and dilution training panel), the close before each results release (the results-move training rows; v1 used the latest month-end) and each company's latest day (the product). Four definition changes from v1: values count from their `known_at` (v1: the filing date); a share count is put in the row date's shares with the split factors (v1 ignored a split after the count, so market value was off until the next report); short-selling ratios use trailing windows (v1's calendar months look ahead from a mid-month row); `months_since_report` counts 20-F and 40-F reports too | A full daily feature table (about 40 million rows); v1's month-end panel only | The same definitions serve training and the product at a fraction of the size; each change removes a look-ahead or an error, and all come before any v2 label or model result |

| V2-15 | **Financial values with impossible dates are left out of `core.financial_fact`** (1,163 of 16.1 million: a period ending more than 31 days after the filing that reports it, or before 1950, such as 2201 typed for 2015); staging keeps them as reported | Keep them; repair the year | They broke the date arithmetic, and guessing the intended year is not safe |
| V2-16 | **Daily SEC data comes through the nightly master index of each filing day**: per day, the headers of the filings whose details are used, the financial values of that day's periodic filers and the history of new registrants; **Form 3, 4 and 5 filings are fetched as full submissions and parsed into the quarterly data sets' tables**, a data set's version winning once published, with a table of differences (feature 001, research R2–R5) | Download the bulk `submissions` and `companyfacts` copies every day (about 3 GB a day); wait for the quarterly Form 4 data sets (up to three months late); the "latest filings" feed | About 600 small requests a day, a complete list per day, and insider trades in the tracker the evening after they are filed; the parser is checked against a past quarter's data set (24–30 Sep 2026: all 1,973 filings in both agree once values are compared at the data sets' precision; the data sets keep shares and prices to two decimals, rounded half up, the filings in full) |
| V2-17 | **Prices update through a short daily window for every listed symbol** (Yahoo: batches of 200, the last 10 days; Tiingo: new days per ticker while the plan lasts), **with a full history refetched when a window shows a split or dividend**; per ticker the newest full history plus later windows make the series | Refetch every full history daily; append windows forever without refetching | Adjusted closes change for the whole past after a split or dividend; a window alone would leave the old adjustment |
| V2-18 | **A listed company's own ticker (in the SEC's latest list) is accepted from either price source on dates alone**, and its history starts at the ticker's current listing (the later of the listing start and the day after another company last used the symbol) | v1's name and exchange identity checks for every ticker | The SEC list is the identity; renamed companies and foreign ADRs failed the name check and went unpriced (78 Tiingo-only, most of 956 price-only stocks) |
| V2-19 | **The daily update re-stages only sources with new files and rebuilds every layer above staging in full**, recorded in `ops` and checked by `sentinel verify` (a full rebuild compared table by table) and the truncation test | Incremental inserts per layer | Full rebuilds of the upper layers take minutes and cannot drift from a rebuild; incremental code would need its own proof of equality |
| V2-20 | **The schedule is a Windows Task Scheduler task** (weekdays at 10:00 Berlin time, when the previous US day is complete; run after a missed start, one at a time) that starts WSL and runs `sentinel update --scheduled`, which puts itself under the 5 GB memory cap without swap | A systemd timer or cron inside WSL | WSL is usually shut down when no terminal is open, so a timer inside it would not fire; the update catches up missed days itself |
| V2-22 | **A foreign filer's market value is blank** (`feature.snapshot`), as risk_params v5 requires: its share count is of ordinary shares while its US price is often an ADR's (researcher, 30 Oct; found by the EDA, 004) | Convert by the ADR ratio | ADR ratios are not modelled; the app showed one airline at \$25 trillion and TSMC at \$11 trillion. 26,048 volatility rows and 775 of 1,327 foreign companies' cards had a value |
| V2-23 | **A share count typed in wrongly is left out** of `core.shares_outstanding` and kept with its reason in `core.shares_rejected` (`share_typos` in `core/filings.py`), judged only by what was known when it was filed: zero or below; a cover-page count 100× or more away from the same filing's weighted-average count; otherwise 100× or more away from the company's last accepted count (split-adjusted) unless the market value stayed within 10× (a real share issue or reverse split moves the price the other way; without prices, the next filing at the same level confirms). It applies before the labels: the dilution definition is unchanged, its input is cleaned (researcher, 30 Oct) | A market-value ceiling (\$5 trillion); comparing with the next count too (look-ahead) | Rejected: 6,686 counts not positive (1,096 companies), 5,519 far from the same filing's average (1,720), 1,670 jumps (585). Named checks: Legg Mason's 112 billion shares (2014–15) and Lamar's 2014 counts are left out; market values above \$5 trillion fell from 215 to 0 rows in the volatility set |
| V2-24 | **An insider trade line with a typed-in price or size has no dollar value** (`value_usd` missing, `value_typo` set; the line and its shares stay): worth more than the whole company (close on or before the filing date × the share count known by then), or an open-market purchase or sale priced 5,000× or more away from that close (researcher, 30 Oct) | 10× (flags share classes: Berkshire's class A trades at about 1,500× its class B; and every \$0 award or gift) | 6,258 lines of 527 companies (2,101 open-market). | V2-26 | **Price-only stocks are eligible for the volatility question** when they pass the price rules (close at least \$1, \$10,000 a day, 63 days of prices, not a fund): `eligible_volatility` in `feature.snapshot`; the other questions keep the active-filer rule (researcher, 30 Oct; decided before any model result; risk_params v5 unchanged, the general `eligible` stays) | Keep the active-filer rule for all questions | Constitution II: no question's universe is limited by another question's data, and volatility needs only prices. | V2-27 | **Foreign filers are left out of the dilution question** (`eligible_dilution` in `feature.snapshot`): the dilution model (007) does not train, test or score on rows with `foreign_filer`, and the app shows no dilution chance for them (researcher, 30 Oct; decided before any model result).
