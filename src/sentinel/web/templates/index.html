How to read the risk bands

How far to trust it

Prices are adjusted for stock splits. Share counts and financial values are shown as first reported, never revised later, so the forecast only uses what was known at the time.

Data: SEC EDGAR (filings, financial statements, Form 4), Yahoo Finance and Tiingo (prices), FINRA (short-sale volume). Updated every weekday.
